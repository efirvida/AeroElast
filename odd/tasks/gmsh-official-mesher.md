# gmsh as the only blade mesher: wire it into the framework, then retire NuMAD (and the windio2 mesher)
Make the gmsh skin mesher the single blade mesher of the framework (runner, config, FSI), but only after it is shown to be the better mesher and after the remaining differences against the references are shown to be physical or numerical, not mesh. Only then are the other meshers removed. Follows odd/tasks/gmsh-gap-reduction.md (L3 closure proposal, L6 acceptance) and odd/tasks/gmsh-store-campaign.md. Runner: scripts/aeroenv.sh; SLURM --ntasks only.
