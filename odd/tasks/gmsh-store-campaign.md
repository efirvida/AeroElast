# gmsh mesher: blade store tests, structural response vs the v1 mesher and the previous v2 mesher (IEA-15)
Run every blade test of the validation store with the gmsh mesher and decide whether the structural response got better or worse, before moving on to FSI. Follows odd/tasks/gmsh-direct-physical-tests.md (VERDICT) and odd/tasks/v2-validation-store.md. Runner: scripts/aeroenv.sh; SLURM --ntasks only.

## Specs
- S1 (user, verbatim): "mezclamos despues quiero ejecutar todos los test del store que sean refrente a la pala con el nuevo mallador, y ver si mejoramos o empeoramos la respuesta estructural, para luego continuar a FSI"
- S2 (scope, derived): the blade tests of the store are the v1 groups 32, 34, 38, 39, 40, 41, 42 (tests/validation/blade/test_iea15mw_*.py, v1 NuMAD mesher, baselines already in the store) and their v2 counterparts, pending groups 61-67 (tests/validation/blade/v2/test_v2_iea15mw_*.py, mesher chosen with V2_BLADE_MESHER). All are IEA-15 at element size 0.25 (certified for the IEA-15, gmsh-validation-15-22 L22). Groups 20-25 (anchor beam, laminate, shell benchmarks, composite beam, mass matrix, blade-vs-CCX) are element/benchmark level and do not depend on the blade mesher; not rerun here.
- S3 (design, fixed before measuring): the same v2 test files are run with V2_BLADE_MESHER=windio2 (previous v2 mesher, control) and V2_BLADE_MESHER=gmsh, same commit, same environment, same tolerances. The v1 numbers are the store baselines of groups 32-42 (not rerun; their source digest is recorded in the store). "Better / worse" is read per row against the row's own independent reference (BeamDyn, OpenFAST MBC3, NREL/TP-5000-75698, the declared mass table) and its own tolerance, as |residual| of gmsh vs |residual| of the control and of v1; a difference below the run-to-run noise of the control (two identical runs) is "same". No tolerance is changed.
- S4 (AGENTS.md physics discipline): negative and null results are reported; a row that cannot be measured is reported as such.

- S5 (user, verbatim, added mid-campaign): "y recuerda agregar las otras palas al store tambien para probar reproducibilidad"
